1. What the app does

The app downloads maps published by third parties and copies, replaces and deletes map files on a Garmin watch connected to the phone with a USB cable, using the watch’s standard file transfer mode (MTP). It is an independent app: it is not made, approved or supported by Garmin.

2. Use at your own risk

  • Copying, replacing and deleting files changes the contents of your watch. Before making changes, keep copies of important maps (the app offers “Save copy to phone”) and back up the watch’s data, such as activities and settings, through Garmin’s own services.

  • Maps come from third parties (such as OpenTopoMap, Freizeitkarte OSM, UMP-pcPL, OpenMTBMap and VeloMap) and may be incomplete, outdated or wrong. Do not rely on them as your only means of navigation; outdoors, always carry other means of orientation and judge the conditions yourself.
